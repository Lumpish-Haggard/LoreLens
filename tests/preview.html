Chapter 412 — The River of Time

Gu Changge stood at the edge of the River of Time, watching the current fold back on itself. Somewhere beneath it, the Immortal Ascension Ritual was still turning, as it had turned for an age before he was born.

Chan Hongyi and Tao Yao could be seen in different segments of that river, each convinced the other was the illusion. Gu Changge did not interfere. The truths that Chan Hongyi and Tao Yao saw were both true, which was the joke of the place.

Yue Mingkong watched him from the far bank. She had followed him here across three lifetimes, and Yue Mingkong had never once asked him to explain himself — which he had always taken as the greater courtesy.

The Immortal Ascension Ritual shuddered. Somewhere upstream, the Gu Family had begun to move.
